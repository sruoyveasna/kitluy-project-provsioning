# Code of Conduct

KitLuy Suite is developed by HET and its authorized collaborators.

- Be respectful and professional in all project spaces.
- Communicate in Khmer or English; both are first-class project languages.
- Assume good intent; resolve disagreements with evidence and the authority
  order defined in `docs/authority/`.
- Report conduct concerns to the project owner
  ([REQUIRED: designated conduct contact — owner decision pending]).
