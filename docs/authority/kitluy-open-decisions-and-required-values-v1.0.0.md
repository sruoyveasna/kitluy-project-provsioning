### Device credential lifecycle additions (WS-11-T003 Step 4, 2026-07-29)

| ID        | Open decision or required value | State |
| --------- | ------------------------------- | ----- |
| KLREQ-031 | **No owner policy governs device private-key retention or destruction.** A renewal supersedes the previous generation's key, and nothing rules on how long that key is kept, what suspends its destruction, or who may authorize it. Destruction is IRREVERSIBLE and it is not revocation: destroying a key does not revoke the credential it backed, and retiring an overlapped credential does not destroy a key. Three distinct controls, and the missing one must not be substituted by either of the others | OPEN — destruction is SPECIFIED and BLOCKED. `kitluy_devices.key_destruction_policy` ships with `destruction_enabled` false, both retention periods NULL and the missing decision NAMED; two CHECK constraints refuse to enable destruction without an approving decision AND both retention periods, so it cannot be turned on by halves. `advanceDeviceCredentialLifecycle` evaluates eligibility and returns `KEY_DESTRUCTION_NOT_AUTHORIZED` for a fully unblocked key, destroying nothing. NO retention duration was invented. `[REQUIRED: device_key_destruction_owner_decision — minimum retention before a superseded private key may be destroyed at all; recovery retention holding it long enough that an interrupted renewal can still be reconciled; what suspends destruction under incident or legal hold and who declares it; who may authorize destruction at the provider; whether destruction is automatic once eligible or requires named operator approval; and what evidence must be recorded to prove a key was destroyed and must survive it]` |
