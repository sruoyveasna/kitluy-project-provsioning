## Material limitation — read with the status claim

Plant production stages (`washing`, `drying`, `pressing`, `qa_packaging`) have
**no approved Edge route and no canonical RBAC key** — independently confirmed
absent from both the RBAC CSV and `EDGE_ROUTES`. Consequently a Booking created
purely through Hub commands **cannot reach READY**, and **no end-to-end T1→T4
lifecycle exists through the Hub command surface alone.** In the reviewer's
probe those stages could only be advanced by asking the canonical engine for
each edge and writing the projection **out of band**.

This is a correctly-fenced gap rather than a defect — inventing a permission key
would violate CLAUDE.md rules 7 and 9 — but it must not be understated. It is
registered as KLREQ-028 and is ruled together with KLREQ-024.
